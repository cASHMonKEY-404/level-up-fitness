LEVEL UP — proportion-refined exercise pictograms

12 original monochrome SVG icons plus a labeled vector preview.

A shared adult rig gives every pose the same bone lengths: 25-unit head height, 57-unit shoulder-to-hip length, 34-unit upper arms, 31-unit forearms, 45-unit thighs, and 44-unit shins. The neutral standing figure is approximately 7.5 heads tall, within a typical adult proportion range. Bent and seated poses naturally have a shorter overall silhouette. Apparent shoulder/hip widths are reduced in side views.

These remain simplified exercise identifiers, not anatomical diagrams or form instructions. Each pose is built from the same proportions rather than independently stretching the limbs.
